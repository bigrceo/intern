# Intern · how it works (15 s, 1080×1080, 60 fps, Remotion)

Mechanic in 3 lines:
1. You write a job in one sentence; it becomes a plan your AI intern runs on your schedule.
2. Your staked $ORBIO earns CREDIT every hour, and that CREDIT pays for the AI. No card.
3. It asks before it acts, and every job leaves a public receipt on Robinhood Chain.
